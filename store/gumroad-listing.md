### Description (paste into the rich-text description)

**You are further along than you think.**

Open almost any task system on a Sunday night and it greets you with a number
you owe. Seven tasks open. Three things unsorted. Something overdue, in red, at
the top. That is a guilt machine, and guilt machines get abandoned in month
three — not because they are missing a feature, but because you never see the
progress you actually made.

NAZZIM opens the other way round. The first thing on the page is what you
finished this week, how far your goals have moved, and how many weeks you kept
the habit. Then a bar for every day you completed something. Only after that
does today's work appear.

**Nothing on that page is typed by hand.** Every number, bar and row is computed
from work you already entered. The only upkeep the system asks for is thirty
minutes on a Sunday.

---

**What you get**

- **14 pages**, every one designed — not one bare page after the home screen.
- **9 linked databases** — Goals, Areas, Projects, Tasks, Inbox, Knowledge,
  Notes, Resources, Reviews. Related to each other, not nine separate lists.
- **20 charts and rollups**, all computed. Completion by day, goal progress
  against your own metric and your own deadline, weekly review scores.
- **A Command Center** that answers *what should I do right now* using context,
  energy and time available — not just priority.
- **An Attention column** on Projects that tells you which project is stalled
  and why: no next action, overdue tasks, or nothing open at all.
- **A capture box on the home page** so an idea never costs you a page load.
- **Colour-coded zones** — Daily, Build, Think, Rhythm, System — carried through
  every page cover so you always know where you are.
- **Sample data included**, so you see the system alive on first open instead of
  a page of zeroes. Delete it whenever you like; nothing breaks when it goes.

---

**Built on PARA and GTD, without the homework**

Goals are what you want to be true by a date. Areas are what you maintain
forever. Projects are finite efforts that deliver goals. Tasks are single
actions that deliver projects. Knowledge runs alongside: resources are what
other people made, notes are what you thought, and a note that produces no task
is storage.

You do not have to learn that to use it. Start Here is seven numbered steps and
takes about seven minutes.

---

**Reads on a phone**

Every view was built to be legible on a phone screen — task title first, then
the chips. No app to install, no sync to configure. It is Notion.

---

**Who this is not for**

It is built for one person, not a team. There are no assignees, no approvals, no
shared workspaces. If you need to run a company on it, buy something else.

## What only you can do

1. **Create the Gumroad account** and connect a payout method. Gumroad needs a
   bank account or PayPal before it will release money; do this first because
   verification can take a day or two.
2. **Produce the two Notion duplicate links.** In Notion: open the root page →
   Share → Publish → turn on **Allow duplicate as template** → copy the link.
   One for `NAZZIM Pro`, one for the Free root page. I cannot create these —
   publishing a page to the web is an account-level action on your side.
3. **Duplicate the templates into a clean workspace and buy your own product**
   before you announce it. Buy it, click the link, duplicate it, and use it for
   a day. Every delivery problem shows up in that hour and nowhere else.
4. **Upload the five images** from `brand/gumroad/` in the order above.
5. **Set the price** and confirm the currency. The figure discussed elsewhere in
   this repo was 11.150 OMR; $29 is the nearest round equivalent, and USD is
   what the Gumroad audience expects.
6. **Check Gumroad's current fees before you set the price.** Gumroad charges a
   platform fee plus payment processing, and takes a larger share on sales it
   sends you through Discover. The rates have changed more than once — read the
   current pricing page rather than trusting any number quoted here.
7. **Turn on Discover** for both listings and fill in the tags. Discover is the
   reason to be on Gumroad rather than only Shopify: it is a search surface with
   buyers already on it. Your Shopify store has no equivalent.
8. **Decide what happens to the Shopify listing.** Running both splits your
   reviews and your social proof across two platforms, and reviews are what sell
   the next copy. Pick one as the storefront of record.
