# NAZZIM vs. heyismail "Second Brain Pro+"

The competitor the user asked NAZZIM to beat. Gumroad and notion.com are both
blocked by this session's egress proxy, so the feature inventory below is read
from a high-resolution screenshot of their four dashboards plus what public
search returns. Anything I could not verify is marked as such.

---

## The first thing to understand: the link is the free one

`heyismail.gumroad.com/l/PARA` is the **free** template. Their own promotion
reads *"Get my FREE Second Brain Notion Template."* It carries **23,000+
downloads and 334+ five-star ratings**. "Second Brain Pro+" is the paid upsell
sitting behind it.

So the thing being competed with is not really a product. It is a funnel:

```
free PARA template  →  23k downloads  →  334 public five-star ratings
                    →  social proof   →  paid Pro+
```

NAZZIM currently has a paid product and no funnel. That asymmetry matters more
than any feature on the list below, and no amount of building fixes it.

## What they have that NAZZIM does not

### 1. Quick Capture buttons — the real one

Every dashboard of theirs opens with a stack of one-click buttons: *New Task,
New Project, New Note, New Goal, New Resource*. It is the most visible thing in
all four screenshots and it appears on every page.

**NAZZIM cannot ship these.** Notion buttons cannot be created through the API
that built this system — verified, not assumed. The closest NAZZIM gets is the
**New** button that every database view already carries, which is the same job
in one more click, plus a documented 30-second procedure in Settings & Help for
adding real buttons by hand.

This is a genuine loss on first impression. It is not a loss in use.

### 2. A Finance Hub

Seven entries in their sidebar: Main Dashboard, Accounts, Incomes, Expenses,
Budgets, Subscriptions, Transfers. "Finance" is in their product title.

NAZZIM has no finance anything in Pro. This is the only substantial *feature*
gap, and it is buildable.

### 3. Learning System and Reading Tracker

Two dedicated trackers. NAZZIM's Resources database does the reading-tracker job
(`Status`: To Review → Reviewed → Reference) but is not branded or specialised
as one.

### 4. Social proof

23,000 downloads. 334 five-star ratings. NAZZIM has zero of both. This is the
widest gap in the entire comparison and it is not a design problem.

## What NAZZIM has that they do not

Read from their screenshots — their dashboards show status chips and counts, and
nothing that computes a judgement.

| NAZZIM | What it does that theirs does not appear to |
| --- | --- |
| `Attention` on Projects | Says **why** a project is stuck: no next action, no open tasks, or overdue tasks. Empty means healthy. Theirs shows a status chip you set by hand. |
| `Health` on Projects and Goals | Compares progress against *elapsed time*, so a goal slipping says so months before its deadline. |
| Goal `Progress` fallback | Uses your metric when a target is set, the share of completed projects when it is not — so a goal is never silently unmeasurable. |
| Command Center | A whole page that proves what is wrong, rather than listing what exists. |
| `Action Count` on Notes | How many tasks a note produced. Closes the knowledge→action loop; a note at zero for months was entertainment. |
| Analytics | Twenty live charts computed from real rows. Nothing comparable is visible in their four dashboards. |
| Four rhythms + scored weekly review | Daily/Weekly/Monthly/Yearly named, costed and located, with a 1–10 execution score that trends over time. |
| Formula-driven dates | `Timeline` and `Today Tier` recompute daily, so Today and Overdue never go stale. |
| Settings & Help | Names every Notion limitation instead of hiding it — buttons, automations, recurrence, no knowledge graph. |

**The one-line difference:** theirs is a very well-organised filing system.
NAZZIM is an instrument — it computes judgements about your work and tells you
what is wrong without being asked.

## Where they beat NAZZIM on craft

Being honest about the screenshots rather than defensive:

- **Airier.** Generous whitespace, light ground, wide margins. NAZZIM is denser.
- **Guidance is always visible.** Every section carries *What It Is:* and *How
  to Use:* in italics, right there. NAZZIM deliberately folds this into toggles
  after the user asked for less clutter — a defensible choice, but theirs is
  friendlier on the first thirty seconds.
- **Sidebar navigation.** A narrow left column of grouped links reads like an
  app. NAZZIM uses a horizontal bar, which wraps on a phone.
- **Methodology branding.** Separate pages literally titled *PARA Dashboard* and
  *GTD Dashboard*. NAZZIM **is** PARA and GTD throughout, but never says so where
  a buyer scanning a listing would see it. Pure marketing, zero build cost.

## The finding that argues against copying them

From the 2026 market research on what actually sells:

> The format that consistently outperforms is the focused single-purpose
> system, not the all-in-one life OS — a weekly review template that does one
> job well outsells a "second brain" that promises everything.

NAZZIM's tagline is **"Turn information into execution."** That is a focused
promise. Bolting on Accounts, Incomes, Expenses, Budgets, Subscriptions and
Transfers makes it an all-in-one, which is the category the research says
underperforms — and it dilutes the one sentence that makes NAZZIM easy to sell.

## Recommendation

1. **Do not chase the Finance Hub for parity alone.** Build it only as a
   deliberate move into the all-in-one category, and if it is built, build it the
   NAZZIM way — three databases (Accounts, Transactions, Budgets) rather than
   seven, with Transactions related to **Areas**, so the system can answer *which
   part of my life costs the most*. That is an answer their seven separate
   databases cannot give, and it turns a parity feature into a differentiator.
2. **Ship a free tier.** The Free edition is already specified in Settings —
   Inbox, Tasks, Projects, Areas, Today. It exists on paper. Shipping it is the
   single highest-return competitive action available, because it attacks the
   thing that actually makes them win: the funnel.
3. **Say PARA and GTD out loud** on the storefront and on Start Here. Free, and
   it is already true.
4. **Accept the buttons gap** and turn it into a line of copy: every view has a
   New button, and Settings shows how to add real ones in thirty seconds.
